# Experiment B — source-method crosswalk, Stage 1

**Modern-period partial-method reproduction / hypothesis check. Historical source-era replication is not executed.** Methods below are grounded in §§1,4–6 of the two locally approved B v1 documents under `docs/specs/`. Their verified-source summaries are the basis of this stage; no new full-text source-paper verification or paywall access was undertaken. Exact RV normalization and original state calendar grouping remain unresolved.

References: Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (2018), *Market intraday momentum*, JFE129(2),394–414, [publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2018.05.009); Limkriangkrai, Chai & Zheng (2023), *Market intraday momentum: APAC evidence*, PBFJ80,102086, [publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pacfin.2023.102086). These links identify the papers; this session does not claim independent inspection of new article text.
